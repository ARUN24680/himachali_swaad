### 🖨️ Printing Guidelines for Physical Jars:
* **Side Label:** Wrap-around size `2.5 inch × 7 inch` on **BOPP Vinyl with Matte Lamination** (oil-proof & water-resistant).
* **Cap Lid Sticker:** `50 mm` or `55 mm` diameter circular die-cut on **BOPP Vinyl Matte**.
