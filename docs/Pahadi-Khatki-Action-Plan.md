## 1. Business Model & Division of Responsibilities

**Pahadi Khatki** is a Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) food venture delivering authentic, handcrafted Himachali non-veg pickles (Chicken, Mutton, Fish) directly from Himachal Pradesh to consumers across India. The venture functions on a lean, two-location partnership model:

### 🏔️ Himachal Pradesh Team (Partner Desk)
* **Authentic Cooking:** Sourcing local fresh chicken, pure mustard oil, and authentic pahadi spices; cooking in small, consistent batches.
* **Quality Control:** Ensuring proper cooking, exact bone-meat trimming, oil coverage, and shelf stability.
* **Bottling & 4-Layer Packaging:** Filling glass jars to accurate 250g net weight, sealing with foam wads & heat shrink bands.
* **Order Fulfillment:** Boxing jars, pasting generated AWB shipping labels, and handing parcels to the courier pickup person at the doorstep.

### 💻 Chennai Command (Arun Desk)
* **Digital Infrastructure:** Building & maintaining the online storefront, partner mobile order screen, and database.
* **Marketing & Sales:** Running social media campaigns, Instagram reels, meta ads, and driving daily website orders.
* **Logistics Integration:** Managing Shiprocket automated courier assignments, tracking links, and customer address verification.
* **Finance & Banking:** Razorpay payment gateway integration, customer support, and weekly 50/50 profit distributions.

> **🤝 Profit Sharing Principle: Flat 50% / 50% Net Split**  
> All net profits will be split equally (50% Himachal / 50% Chennai).  
> **Net Profit = Customer Revenue minus direct expenses** (Raw Material + Packaging Materials + Courier Delivery + Payment Gateway fee). Neither partner takes a salary during the initial launch phase; all profits flow equally.

## 2. Critical Misunderstandings & Rules to Follow ("Kya Nahi Karna Hai")

During planning, 3 major misunderstandings were identified. If these are not corrected immediately, they can lead to legal notices from the Food Ministry (FSSAI), consumer court complaints, or ruined customer trust.

### 🚨 Rule #1: Net Weight vs Courier Gross Weight (Strict Legal Rule)
* **The Misunderstanding:** Thinking that the jar should weigh 200g or 250g including the packaging box and jar weight.
* **The Law (Legal Metrology & FSSAI):** When a food label says **"Net Quantity: 250g"**, it legally means **only the edible food inside the jar** (chicken meat + mustard oil + spices). It does NOT include the glass jar, cap, or outer box.
* **How to Handle Courier:** A glass jar weighs ~180g, food weighs 250g, and corrugated box + bubble wrap weighs ~70g. Total shipping weight will be **~500g (0.5 kg)**. In Shiprocket, we will always book the **0.5 kg slab**. The courier company charges for 500g, while the customer gets full 250g of fresh pickle.

### 🫙 Container Selection: Glass vs Plastic
* ❌ **Never Use Plastic Jars (PET):**
  * Non-veg pickles use hot mustard oil, salt, and vinegar (acetic acid).
  * Acid reacts with plastic polymers, creating health hazards and synthetic off-flavors.
  * Plastic absorbs pungent meat odors that never wash out.
  * Plastic screw threads expand in warm transit, causing guaranteed oil leakage.
* ✅ **Always Use Glass Jars + Air Wrap:**
  * Food-grade soda lime glass jars with lug caps & plastisol gaskets.
  * 100% non-reactive: Preserves authentic mountain taste for 6+ months.
  * High customer perceived value; matches premium ₹399 price point.
  * **Breakage Protection:** Do not use thermocol (messy and brittle). Wrap with **50 GSM 3-layer bubble wrap** or **air column sleeves**.

### ⚖️ Rule #2: FSSAI vs GST (Clarity on Registrations)
* **FSSAI Registration (Mandatory Before Launch):** Food safety registration is non-negotiable. We will apply for **Basic Registration (Form A)** via the FoSCoS portal. It costs only **₹100 per year**, is approved in 3–5 working days, and requires only the Himachal partner's Aadhaar card and kitchen electricity bill/rent agreement.
* **GST Registration (NOT Required Right Now):** Under Indian tax laws, intra-state and e-commerce threshold for small home food makers selling directly via their own website is exempt up to **₹40 Lakhs annual turnover**. We do not need GST initially, saving unnecessary monthly accountant filing fees.

## 4. Safe 4-Layer Packaging Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

* **Step 1: Food Filling & Headspace Inspection**  
  Leave 0.5 inches of headspace at the top of the glass jar. Ensure pickle pieces are completely submerged in mustard oil (oil acts as a natural preservative preventing fungal growth). Thoroughly wipe the glass neck and screw threads with a clean dry cotton cloth before placing the cap.
* **Step 2: Foam Wad Gasket & Lug Cap Tightening**  
  Place an induction foam wad gasket inside the metal lug cap. Screw the cap on tightly until fully locked. The plastisol liner compressed against the rim creates an airtight barrier that stops liquid oil from creeping through threads.
* **Step 3: PVC Heat Shrink Sleeve (Tamper & Leak Lock)**  
  Slide a clear perforated PVC shrink band over the cap and neck. Use a standard electric hair dryer set on high heat for 3 to 5 seconds. The plastic will shrink tightly around the rim, forming a solid physical lock that guarantees the cap cannot loosen during transit vibrations.
* **Step 4: Cushioning & 3-Ply Box Enclosure**  
  Wrap the glass jar with 3 full rotations of 50 GSM bubble wrap (or slide into an inflatable air column bag). Place inside the snug 3-ply corrugated box. Fill any void spaces with crumpled kraft paper so the jar does not move. Tape with reinforced tape, paste the Shiprocket AWB barcode on top, and add a "FRAGILE / GLASS" sticker.
